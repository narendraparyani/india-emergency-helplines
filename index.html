🚆 Commuter & Travel Safety Helplines
🚕 In a Cab or Auto & Feeling Unsafe? Quick Safety Protocol:
  • Dial 112 Immediately: Keep the line connected. Indian CAD centers capture cell tower coordinates even if you are unable to talk.
  • Hit In-App Emergency (Uber / Ola / Rapido): Immediately sends telemetry data to safety response teams and bridges to local police.
  • WhatsApp Live Location: Share continuous live location with family/friends.
  • Child-lock Alert: If you suspect the rear child lock is engaged, demand to alight at a well-lit junction immediately.
